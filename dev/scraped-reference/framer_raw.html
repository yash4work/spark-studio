Frequently asked

Frequently asked

Can you customize any Framer template?

Yes. We adapt and customize any official Framer marketplace template or third-party theme—adjusting brand palettes, typography, custom CMS fields, and third-party integrations.

What is the typical turnaround time?

Template customizations are completed in 3 to 5 business days. Bespoke website builds take 2 to 3 weeks depending on scope.

How do you optimize Framer site speed?

We eliminate render-blocking fonts, compress assets to AVIF/WebP, and streamline responsive breakpoint trees to consistently achieve 95+ scores on Google PageSpeed Insights.

How do we get started?

Email your project brief directly to sparkstudio0@outlook.com. We review your requirements and respond within 24 hours with a fixed quote and start date.
